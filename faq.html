FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to common questions about buying, selling, and staying safe on TrustHub Africa.

What is TrustHub Africa?

A verified marketplace where buyers and sellers trade safely across Africa.

Who can use the platform?

Students, artisans, small businesses, and verified individuals can buy or sell.

How do I get verified?

Complete registration details and follow the verification prompts in your account.

How are payments protected?

Payments are processed through the platform and held until delivery confirmation.

Can I track my order?

Yes, order tracking is available for all supported delivery routes.
