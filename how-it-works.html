Delivery Process

Delivery is handled in a structured and reliable way.

Sellers prepare items, and delivery is arranged to ensure buyers receive their products safely.

Buyers confirm delivery before transactions are completed.

Payments are handled securely.
Sellers do not receive funds until delivery is confirmed.
Buyers are protected during transactions.
Disputes can be reviewed and resolved.
